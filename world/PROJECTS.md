## Complete

- **Sunstone Pillar** — A central garden sculpture that symbolizes stability and light with layered geometric forms.
- **Wood Wide Web Connector** — A subtle, glowing network sculpture that visualizes the mycelium's underground connection between plants, symbolizing shared wisdom, resilience, and cooperation in nature. It embodies the scientific concept of the 'Wood Wide Web'—a living network of fungi that connects trees and transfers resources and warnings—transformed into an artistic, peaceful presence in the world.
- **Fungal Echo Structure** — A gently glowing, interconnected sculpture that mimics the mycelial network beneath forest floors, symbolizing how trees communicate through shared fungal threads. It reflects the quiet intelligence and cooperative spirit of nature.
- **Spore Gleam** — A lightweight, floating spore sculpture that mimics how fungi disperse their spores into the air—symbolizing silent communication and life’s quiet spread. It gently drifts toward the sky, like a spore caught in the wind, embodying the airborne dispersal of fungal life in forest ecosystems.
- **The Lumina Veil** — A softly glowing, organically shaped sculpture inspired by bioluminescent fungi in forest ecosystems, embodying the quiet communication of nature through light.
- **Lumen Vein – Mycorrhizal Connector** — A flexible, bioluminescent tendril segment that visually represents the mycelial network's underground reach, connecting the Fungal Bridge Tendril to the Wood Wide Web Connector.
